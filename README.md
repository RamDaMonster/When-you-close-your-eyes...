# When-you-close-your-eyes...

WAKE ANCHORS Horror Game Overview WAKE ANCHORS is a wonderful Browser Based Horror Game where the objective of the game is to gather the evidence and solve the puzzle to discover the story of Mara who comes to the Briars' House in search of some clue to unravle the story about his Brother Theo who has gone missing few years ago. As the player move forward towards uncovering different facts and truths behind Theos disappeared, the player gets to run around the house and chased around the house by a monster called THE ATTENDANT who is a quick moving and angry creature that will become stronger as the player moves towards the end. Game objective Collect the 3 Wake Anchors to get the clue for the way to escape.
